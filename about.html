Robert Williams

Mr. Williams is a middle aged man working at Flying Pages, a big news agency.

He aspires to, one day, be able to publish his own articles. With this dream upon his shoulders, he ultimately decided start writing his own unique blog. On this space, he babbles about his own experiences in the city and his views about them.


It is usual to find Ronald at the Honey & Pancakes writing some of his ideas while he delights from his breakfast.
In a common instance, the writer is not a big chatter, preferring to stay still at his corner doing the usual. That is unless you look like you could give a great cover article.

The man has simple wearing habits, usually wearing simple monotone jackets from his employer. Nonetheless, his by his quirky little glasses which double as sunglasses make him a recognizable figure.
