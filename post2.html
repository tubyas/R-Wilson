Saint Sciurus?
More like, Cursed Sciurus!

25/09/1999

I was staying at this friend's house in Saint Sciuris, making up for lost time. He was acting beyond freakish.
I asked for his mother to no regard. He didn’t remember her name. I questioned “Where is Ms. Florence” as he stared visibly confused. In persistence, I rephrase it like “Your sweet mother, what’s with her?”. There he answered with “Oh! She is on vacation.”.
Now that I rethink the moment, he also acted noticeably out of character once he saw my face at the door. It was like I was a mere stranger. Just after a few moments did he finally recognized my existence. I simply glanced at the situation as one does, “Maybe he just didn't recognized my face, I haven’t seen him in years”.


Throughout the day he seemed surprisingly unorganized, something undefined from my fellow mate.
I remember asking him for a coffee and, despite his recognizable good spirit, he took an abnormal wait to serve me with my well deserved mug of coffee. I heard him all over the kitchen, messing around with his stuff. It appeared as he was in an intensive search for every little thing. Almost as he didn’t sleep under this roof. ㅤ


Eventually, the sun was setting down. It was time to go back to the city.
While we were saying our goodbyes, something otherworldly happened. From somewhere in between the woods of the surrounding forest, a white beam emerged.
Without any time to catch a breath, a white wave of shining fog crossed the heavens. From how fast it appeared, it disappeared. Both of us stand there like a baboons, staring at a nutritious banana.
As lighting had struck into his nevers, my companion went back home exclaiming to me “OH! HOW COULD I FORGET! MY MOM IS SICK AT THE HOSPITAL! I shall call her!”. Just like that, I was left alone at that door, shocked, wondering what possibly could have happened.
Until now I don’t know but I have my suspicions!


Instead of driving back home, I booked a short stay at the nearest motel, the same one I’m writing this from. After getting the keys from the sweet lady who administers the place, I reencountered a few weirdos I had seen earlier the same week.

SOMETHING IS UP IN THIS GODDAMN PLACE! AND I, RONALD WILLIAMS, WILL UNRAVEL IT!

Rest of a good afternoon,
Williams, Ronald
