WELCOME TO NEW LAMBFIELD!

05/09/1999

"The city where you reach your dreams and live experiences like nowhere" or so they said.

Since the first day I moved to this place I saw things that I cannot describe. Everyone here is weird and I feel like there is something beyond my understanding.
Yesterday, while I was taking my trash out, I saw shadowy figure dropping a card at someone's door and simply vanish into dust like nothing happened. This is not just the usual shady people that I saw back in the states, this is beyond freakish.

In my job at Flying Pages I randomly ask some coworkers if they don't feel like there is something beyond in this city. I'm always left with weird stare and an empty sack of words. I'm fed up of this lack of explanation there is about what is happening in these goddam streets. That's why I'm starting this blog. If no one cares to bring to world what is happening in this fucking city, I will do it my self!

Rest of a good afternoon,
Williams, Ronald
