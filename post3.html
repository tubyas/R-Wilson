New Lambfield Thematic Cards!

06/10/1999

Salutations, fellow followers!

Quick update. During my limited non-working routine, I began pitching and designing a pack of cards inspired by the many tales I have read about this mysterious town.
The set is not yet finished! yet I am mesmerized to share with you all the current designs in this digital formation of the designs.

Quickly I aim to rant briefly about my designer. He is indeed a very precious kid, however, he just sent me the backface of the cards and I'm astonished.
Yes, the design isn't at all to question in matters of visual appeal, nonetheless, I can barely read it! It is supposed to be called LAMB KAMB (a really great name I have come up with myself). As you shall observe shortly, the last "m" is running out of the card almost and it is written "LABM" instead of "LAMB"!
I have already sent to print some demonstrations but now I have 3 packs of cards with a major misprint!

Uh... The kid already sent me an email exclaiming how sorry he was but that he couldn't redesign them on time due to some "3D Project". I asked him personally if he was working with that surprising movie of the walking dolls, sadly, he responded that "he wished". Apparently he is working on some "independent commission work" involving a fisherman?
Ah! What the kids get into nowadays.


Concluding, I'm terribly ambarassed for the noticeable mistake at my first product for you'll but here it is a showcase of the cards in a type of memory game.


Rest of a good afternoon,
Williams, Ronald
